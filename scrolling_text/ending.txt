Gaioh perished, Great Axion sank,
and the New Empire Imperium fell.
With that vast crisis over,
humanity entered a new phase.
Against the many threats, the 
UN forces came into prominence.
The drive to unite across
nations surged all at once.
That movement advanced rapidly.
A month later, the three powers
declared an "Earth Federation".
At the end of Multi-Era 0020,
after the World Ruin Incident,
humanity began the path to unity
under the Earth Federation.
As the awaited age neared,
those who fought to win peace
entered their own new battles...
