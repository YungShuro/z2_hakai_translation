The Dark Continent...
Born in southern Africa after
the Great Dimensional Quake, 
it remained shrouded in mystery.
But when a Dimensional Quake
struck, its boundaries stabilized
and it was no longer inaccesible.
Elgan Roddick, envoy of the UN
Peacekeeping Counil sent an 
expedition to investigate.
He already knew.
A new war would begin there...
