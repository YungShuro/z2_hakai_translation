In this world, two Japans exist.
One Japan, since the Great
Dimensional Quake, had avoided
major wars and lived in peace.
The other Japan, after the Far
East Incident 7 years ago, was
ruled by Britannia as 'Area 11'.
Japanese became 'Elevens' and
all but "Honarary Brittanians"
faced systemic discrimination.
In response, some Elevens
formed a resistance and kept
fighting the Britannia Union.
In the Kanto Block, where many
from the Astragius Galaxy lived,
many resistance groups moved
in the shadows of the unrest.
But before the might of the
Brittania Union, a hopeless
reality loomed over the Elevens.
As war spread across the global,
under the thumb of Brittania,
hope for Area 11 seemed lost.
Until that day...
