Celestial Being's emergence
shifted the balance of power.
At the same time, the five
Colony gundams began to act.
Theirs actions spurred tensions
and changes in the world.
The mysterious Dancouga Nova,
aiding the losing sides in
battles, led to prolonged wars.
CB sought to end war, while
Dancouga Nova spread its flames.
Though opposed in nature, their
actions came to shape the world
and the battlefield.
Their meeting was inevitable...
