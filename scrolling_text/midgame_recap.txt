Project Uzume...
A space-time quake in Limonesia
opened a gate to other worlds.
Gaioh, the King of Ruin
and ruler of DBs, declared the
world a stage for his battles.
No one knew the meaning of those
words, not even Gaioh himself.
This 'Calamity Birth' further
destabilized the world. With the
New Imperium spreading war as it
advanced, people responded with
a mix of both fear and resolve.
And so, humanity would be forced
to make some hard decisions...
