Many worlds, many universes...
In other dimensions, they were
called parallel worlds, meant
never to cross. Until that day.
The Great Space-Time Quake...
A vast dimensional tremor
struck one world and shattered
the walls between dimensions.
Countless worlds mixed as one.
Thus the multiverse was born.
New worlds brought new meetings.
New meetings brought new wars.
For long after they were born,
many worlds lay in chaos.
Yet some found new order.
The stage is one world's Earth.
This Earth had two moons
and two Japanese island chains.
After 20 years, it knew order.
Elevators reached into space.
Orbital rings were completed.
Colonies rose at Lagrange.
Yet peace still never came...
The world was split among
three great blocs of nations.
Britannia Union was born
when the Union of Solar Energy
and Free Nations and the 
Britannian Empire, both in North
America, merged into one nation.
The AEU was Europe's union.
The Human Reform League (HRL)
combined China, Asia, and Russia.
These three great powers vied
for control of the world,
while the Colony Nations
Council (CMC) ruled the colonies.
As cold war gripped the three
great powers, small clashes
continued across the world.
Wars, resistance, and terror...
Those from Astragius, who arrived
two years ago, kept their troops
as mercenary groups and helped
spread the flames of war.
Humanity had a common foe.
Mysterious life-forms
from another dimension:
the Dimensional Beasts.
Even as Image attacks waned,
DB attacks numbers rose each year.
With fear and rage still held in
so many hearts, the world turns.
And ahead there waited
a storm of war called change...
