A mysterious space fleet
has arrived in Earth's sphere.
Let us review their story...
Many parallel worlds exist
In one world, Earth was attacked
by giant aliens, the Zentradi, 
and mankind neared extinction.
After fierce battles, peace came.
Humanity survived and began the
Galaxy Colonization Plan to
preserve their own kind, sending 
massive fleets into space to
find a new home.
The 25th Long-Distance Fleet,
Macross Frontier, was one of them.
But a massive space-time quake
Would change their destiny...
